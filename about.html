WHO WE ARE

K.S Enterprises is a trusted manufacturer and exporter of premium-quality herbal products based in Murshidabad, West Bengal, India, committed to supplying natural, authentic and export-quality products to domestic and international markets. Our flagship product, Nagori Ashwagandha Root, sourced from the renowned cultivation regions of Maharashtra and Rajasthan, is recognized for its superior potency, high withanolide content and exceptional quality. We specialize in the sourcing, processing, manufacturing and export of Ashwagandha Root, Ashwagandha Powder, Kapoor Kachri, Raw Jute, Jute Products and other premium herbal products. Every product is carefully selected, processed and packed according to international quality standards before dispatch. Our commitment to quality, transparency, competitive pricing and customer satisfaction has helped us build long-term business relationships with buyers across domestic and international markets.

OUR JOURNEY

K.S Enterprises began its journey in 2017 as A.M Enterprise and later evolved into K.S Enterprises, becoming a trusted supplier of premium herbal, agricultural and natural products through quality assurance, transparent business practices, competitive pricing, reliable supply chains and timely worldwide delivery.
